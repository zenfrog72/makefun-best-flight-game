**Island Courier** is a tropical island-hopping courier flight game. Fly a small
prop plane between five Indonesian-flavoured islands — Pulau Karang, Tanjung
Api, Pulau Lumba, Pulau Karang Kecil and Pulau Badai — across four sorties:
a cargo run, a sunset ring slalom, a low medical drop, and a stormy night
landing on a short lit runway.

Real flight feel: throttle-driven speed, banking turns, stall below 55 kt,
flare on landing, fuel you can only refill at an airport, and damage from
hard landings. Full HUD (airspeed, altimeter, compass tape, fuel, damage,
timer, minimap, target pointer) and hand-synthesised WebAudio engine, wind
and rain sounds. Built with PixiJS (vendored, no network).

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no network.

- **W/S or ↑/↓** throttle · **A/D or ←/→** bank · **I/K** climb/descend
- **Space** drop the medical crate / confirm · **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart mission
